FLOWSHOOTER — recorded audio (there are no synthesized sound effects any more)

Every SFX is a clip from this folder. Drop .wav / .ogg / .mp3 files here and
name them "<bucket>_whatever" — GameFeel buckets them by the prefix:

  parry_        the parry (a sword clash; pitched up as you chain)
  whoosh_soft_  medium-effort movement: perfect hops, edge boosts, double jumps
  whoosh_hard_  high-effort movement: dash, wall kick, corner boost, slam, chaser strike
  cloth_        low-effort movement: jump, slide-jump, double jump
  swing_        sword whiffs           slice_   sword hits / executes
  step_         footsteps              slide_   slide scrape ticks
  leather_      landings               stone_/punch_/metal_/glass_/wood_  impact materials
  boom_ sub_    big hits (crash channel)   projhit_  projectile impacts   shot_  enemy fire
  belt_         grapple                glitch_  hidden tech
  bownock_      an arrow set on the string   bowdraw_  the limbs creaking back   bowshot_  the loose
  tick_ warn_ dud_ ching_ rankup_ rankdown_ flowin_ flowout_ spawn_ stagger_ warp_ portal_ finish_
Older material-named files (impactGlass_*, impactMetal_* ...) are bucketed by the word.

Silent on purpose: plain bhops, slide starts, soft landings (Deadlock rule —
softer whoosh = less effort; the small stuff makes no sound at all).

Installed (all CC0): Kenney Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-fi Sounds, Interface
Sounds; OpenGameArt RPG Sound Pack + Swishes Sound Pack (artisticdude), 20 Sword
Sound Effects (StarNinjas). See LICENSE-*.txt.
