MOMENTUM — THE SCORE
====================

2026-10-08: THE OLD SCORE IS GONE. The recorded loops (city DnB, under electro
house, office house, the vaporwave menu track, the ambient calm/chill loops and
the three boss tracks) were moved, with their .meta files and the full licence
list, to C:\Users\D\MovementShooter\Attic\Music (outside Assets: nothing there
is built or loaded). The director no longer references any of them.

The score is a REMIX (MusicRemix.cs, MusicDirector.cs). Since 2026-10-16 the
LEAD is THE HOTEL ROOM (MusicHotel.cs): a procedural noir-jazz side in the mould
of Hareton Salvanini's "Quarto de Hotel" (1974) — slow, an electric-bass
ostinato, brushed boom-bap, Rhodes, a muted trumpet, strings, tape wobble and
vinyl — thirteen seeded sides across the pools (menu, city, under, office, boss),
with a jazz-hop BED (MusicBeds.cs: the beat hardening, an 808 under the bass,
rolls, brass stabs, the strings swelling; double-time breaks in flow) synthesised
on the lead's own plan. Nothing in the lead or the beds is sampled; music is the
one place synthesis is allowed, every SFX stays a recording.

Resources/Music/jazz/   the GRAMOPHONE: public-domain 78s used only as a rare,
                        low, band-passed layer in the lobby and the calm, and
                        only when MusicDirector.Gramophone is on (off by
                        default). A side whose chart says lead=1 is measured
                        and played as a lead instead (the 2026-10-08 engine).
                        See jazz/README.txt. Public domain, CC0 or CC-BY ONLY.

Gramophone sides (not leads)
----------------------------
  Twelve 1922–1925 sides from the Internet Archive's Great 78 Project, PUBLIC
  DOMAIN in the US (recordings published 1925 or earlier, 17 U.S.C. 1401), fetched
  2026-10-08 with the owner's go-ahead: the full list with every source URL is
  jazz/JAZZ_LICENSES.txt. All twelve carry gramophone=1 in their charts: heard
  only as the gramophone layer when MusicDirector.Gramophone is on. No credit is
  required; the transfers carry no separate claim.

  format for a new entry:
  jazz/<file>   = "<title>" by <artist> (<year>), <source url> — <licence>
                  CREDIT REQUIRED for CC-BY: "<credit line>"

Everything synthesised (the beds, the crackle, the risers, the procedural band)
is this project's own code and needs no credit.
