## What it is
Halcyon Railways' UNDERGROUND: the city's metro, 1939, Deco stations of cream glazed tile with oxblood and brass bands, mosaic station names, brass turnstiles and ticket halls, escalators, platforms with LIVE third rails and TRAINS that come through; the tunnels with their cable trays; ventilation shafts with great fans; lift shafts; and beneath the metro the SEWERS (brick egg-shaped tunnels, weirs, sluice gates, waterfalls, cisterns, black water), the STEAM MAINS (bursting vents), and THE FOUNDATIONS of the House (the old city buried under the new: crypts, buried streets, collapsing floors). It ends at the TERMINUS under the House, where THE STATIONMASTER waits. "The House runs the trains; the trains run on time; nothing is late but you."

## Hazards (environmental, never a kill: they hurt and THROW; every throw through the motor's attributed paths; red = danger on every one)
Third rail: touch = 25 damage + a throw up and away (an electric arc, the hum readable). Trains: telegraphed 3 s ahead (headlamps on the tunnel wall, the horn, the rail hum rising), contact = 40 + a big throw along the train's travel; dodged by recesses, the cable tray, the platform. Steam vents: a cycle (2 s hiss then a 0.4 s burst): the burst launches you 12 m/s up (a timed pad), 15 damage if you stand in it. Waterfalls: push down (a drop you choose). Sluice gates: timed openings (a line that is open 3 s in 8). Collapsing slabs: drop 0.4 s after you land on them and come back after 6 s (a line you cannot stop on). Weirs: slides with lips. Fans: updrafts that lift (free vertical, like pads).
