
Design directive from the user (2026-08-31, milestone 2): slide hopping is fine and helps maintain momentum (especially up ramps), but a perfectly timed bhop must ALWAYS return more momentum and points. General rule: **the harder an input is to time or execute, the more momentum it grants.**

**Why:** it keeps the skill ceiling meaningful — easy tech maintains speed, hard tech is the only way to gain it, so mastery is always rewarded over spam.

**How to apply:** current ladder in [[flowshooter-project]] (all values in MovementConfig): lazy/buffered slide-jump ×1.15 fading to ×1.0 between 12–18 m/s; regular bhop ×1.0 (preserve, no cap); perfect slide-jump (press within 0.12s AFTER slide start, buffered presses excluded) ×1.15 unfaded; perfect bhop (press within ±0.05s of the landing instant, early buffered presses excluded) ×1.18 uncapped — always the best. When adding new mechanics (corner boost, slam storage, etc.), place their reward on this ladder by input difficulty, and make sure buffered/held inputs never qualify for the "perfect" tier — only genuinely timed presses.

**Deadlock terms, the user's definitions (2026-10-08, with Leif's corner-boosting guide; we had them SWAPPED and the lip move was not a vertical converter):** EDGE BOOST = a wall jump/kick performed on the vertical CORNER of a wall (two faces at ~90°: the kick leaves along the sum of both normals, ×1.4). CORNER BOOST = a jump on a 90° EDGE, the lip where a wall meets its top, that converts speed into VERTICAL momentum (perfect window ±60 ms pays the full conversion, lazy pays less; capped at 2× a jump's impulse so a perfect bhop chain still owns horizontal speed; the WALL COMBO ×1.6 after a wall jump belongs to this move). Never swap them back; the tutorial's lip station is the corner boost, a two-wall vertical corner is the edge boost.
