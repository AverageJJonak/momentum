**THE LIVE GUIDE is `art-direction.md` (2026-10-29): the architecture, lighting, atmosphere, city pieces, palette, the beats mapped to the architecture, the kit rule (deco, not brutalist), and the no-DC rule. This file is the history of the look since 2026-10-07.**


**The decision (2026-10-07):** the user found the futuristic cyberpunk look bland; offered Deco/brutalist/gothic/ruin/ink, they chose ART DECO — "I LOVED art deco growing up with Batman the animated series, everything needs to change to that". Direction: DARK DECO, Gotham 1939. The company (Halcyon) is a 1930s corporation; the House is its tower.

**The look in one line:** the world is painted on black paper; light is what is drawn.

**Palette (linear floats):** night sky indigo (0.03,0.04,0.09) to a burnt horizon (0.35,0.16,0.08); clouds slate (0.18,0.2,0.26) with sepia undersides; stone: cream plaster (0.78,0.72,0.6), limestone (0.55,0.52,0.46), charcoal (0.11,0.11,0.13), black marble (0.05,0.05,0.06) with cream veins; metals: brass (0.78,0.58,0.25, metallic 0.9, smooth 0.7), dark bronze (0.35,0.24,0.12), iron (0.16,0.16,0.18); accents: oxblood (0.32,0.08,0.08), mustard (0.7,0.55,0.2); LIGHT: window amber (1.0,0.72,0.38) emissive, lamp cream (1.0,0.9,0.7), searchlight (0.9,0.95,1.0). NO magenta, NO violet, NO green (the Joker's), NO cyan anywhere outside the blade. Day (first act): sepia daylight, muted, long shadows.
**Colour language survives untouched:** cyan = cut (the blade's electric edge only), gold = parry/reset/flow (now the city's gilt and brass), red = danger (exit signs, hazard lamps, the odd red neon word), amber = a unit's own light (incandescent).

**Materials (LevelKitDeco.cs, partial LevelKit):** `Marble` (black, veined), `Terrazzo` (floor: the speed grid stays but its lines are BRASS), `Cream` (plaster), `Limestone`, `Charcoal`, `Fluted` (plaster with vertical ribs every ~0.3 m), `Brick` (running bond, dark mortar), `Brass`, `Bronze`, `Iron` (riveted), `Oxblood`, `FrostGlass` (transparent), `LampCream` / `WindowAmber` (unlit glows), `WoodPanel` (the Wood surface, dark walnut). Generated like every other surface (LevelKitTextures), PBR maps.

**Forms:** SETBACKS (3–5 steps on every tall mass, the top a crown spire with a finial), fluted pilasters, stepped triple cornices, sunburst transoms over doors, chevron and zigzag friezes, porthole windows, tall arched windows with mullions (box-built), flagpoles, water towers on roofs, chimneys and smokestacks, abstract stepped gargoyles at corners, fire escapes (zigzag iron stairs as looks), iron gates, elevated rails on riveted viaducts, zeppelins with lit gondolas and searchlights, clock faces (lit disc, hands), radio masts with red aircraft lamps, bronze statues as stepped abstractions. Interiors: polished black floors with brass grid lines, cream or oxblood walls with fluted pilasters, stepped ceilings, torchiere up-lights and globe pendants, brass rails, elevator dials (arc + needle), round-cornered counters.

**Lighting and grade (WorldLook):** dark by default; windows, lamps, lit fluting and searchlights carry the picture; crushed blacks, warm highlights, slight desaturation, strong vignette, grain. Cel feel via the grade (shadows/midtones/highlights), never a custom shader. HARD LINE's black beads are the ink line of the style and stay.

**Removed from the city (and the levels where they appear):** neon strips (magenta/violet/rose), holo glyph billboards, black-glass curtain walls (→ stone with punched amber windows), pagodas, crystal spires, mushroom towers, tree towers, sky rings, lanterns, castle walls (→ elevated rail viaducts), exoskeleton diagrids, twisted and stacked towers, needles (→ radio masts). KEPT, re-dressed: cathedrals (Gotham gothic), domes (stations, observatories), keeps (→ stone banks with columns), megablocks and arcologies (→ setback masses), expressways (→ elevated rails), the House (→ a Deco crown, gold stepped, its gold mark kept; its black body becomes charcoal stone with fluted shafts and amber window rows).

**Why:** the user's taste; Deco is the architecture of corporate confidence, which is what the villain is; it suits a world of boxes (setbacks, pilasters, chevrons are stacked rectangles).
**How to apply:** every new piece follows this file; verify the city in Tools/MenuPreview; the menu/HUD get a Deco pass under [[ui-house-style-spec]]'s rules (never cyan). Supersedes the cyberpunk and fantasy passes in [[city-art-direction]]. Related: [[art-style]], [[visual-overhaul]].

**Addendum 2026-10-07, after the first renders (user: "too many windows, looks too modern; buildings should come in all sorts of shapes and sizes; change anything futuristic; high society Gotham, not recoloured cyberpunk"):** a lit WINDOW GRID reads as a curtain wall whatever colour it is. Rules from now on: masonry first — a facade is stone with windows punched in it, bays 5–6 m apart with blank piers between, string courses every 4–6 floors, a rusticated base, an attic and a crown; windows ≤ 25% of a shaft's face, side/party walls blind; at night only 25–40% of windows lit, clustered by floor; the lit ones are sparse points of amber, never a grid. MASSING: a sea of 4–12 storey masonry blocks (brownstone rows, cornice blocks, warehouses, theatres with marquees, hotels, department stores) with a few 20–35 storey setbacks and a handful of crowned giants; landmarks placed deliberately (domed city hall, Gothic cathedral, bank temple, railway terminus, bridge towers, gasometer); irregular silhouette, never rings of identical spires. NOTHING FUTURISTIC: no halo rings, lit rings, blade towers, arcologies, sky trams, LED facades, flying cars, holo signs, light-strip roads; air traffic is zeppelins and police blimps with searchlights; elevated railways on masonry/riveted viaducts. The House is a Deco cathedral of commerce (stepped limestone-charcoal shafts, a crown of stacked setbacks and a lantern, the gold mark lit on the crown), not a black monolith with a ring. The route-tower ENVELOPES (OuterTower variants 0, 1, 2, 3, 5) are what RoofKit builds Level 12/13's walkable roofs to and never change: dress them, never reshape them.

**Atmosphere pass (2026-10-08, user: "visually needs to be more sparkle, smog, atmosphere"):** LevelKitSmog.cs (partial LevelKit + SmogSheets, WindowTwinkle, Glints, LampMotes, FleckWind).
