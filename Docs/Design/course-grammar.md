## Principles
1. **One continuous course.** Spawn → finish, 700–1200 m of route, never a sealed space, never kill-to-open, never a door that closes. Thresholds are arches and openings. Checkpoints are invisible line crossings for respawn; the finish portal stays. The only gated spaces in the game are the two boss arenas (L5 Concierge, L10 Foreman) at the END of their courses.
2. **The line is the level.** Every space is designed as LINES through it: a MAIN LINE anyone runs at 8 m/s, and FAST LINES that need earned momentum (a bhop chain over a gap, a wall run into an EDGE BOOST up a vertical corner, a CORNER BOOST onto a lip, a slide-hop under a low beam, a dash-jump across, glass at speed, a grapple-orb swing, a blast jump), each saving metres or height. A missed fast line drops you onto the main line, never into a pit: no kill floors inside a course.
3. **Vertical.** A course climbs and drops; height is a currency (corner boost up a lip, edge boost up a corner, whip a chandelier, slide a banister, drop into a chute). Every hall has two galleries at different heights reachable by tech.
4. **Enemies are fuel, not gates.** Units stand ON the lines where cutting or tackling through them keeps or gives speed: a bellhop on the lip you corner-boost onto, a row of footmen in a gallery to cut through, a page over a gap to parry-pogo, a doorman in a choke you edge-boost around, a bouncer at a slide's exit. Never waves, never arenas (bosses excepted). Killing is optional; the score's combat part counts kills along the way.
5. **No stops.** Nothing asks the player to stand still: no terminals, no waits, no gates, no lifts you ride (lifts are climbable shafts), no lane narrower than 6 m, no blind 90° turn at speed without a banked wall or a kick wall to carry it. The Ward's lesson (L1) is the one pause: its seal drops on the FIRST punch that connects, not on kills.
6. **Readable at 30 m.** The next line is visible: HARD LINE's lit floor edges run along the fast lines; a BRASS strip marks a lip you can corner-boost; a CREAM flute marks a kick wall or an edge-boost corner. State, never instructions.
7. **Respawn moving.** A respawn puts you at the last checkpoint with run speed along the line; units ahead are as you left them.

## Spaces (all OPEN; built on the +z Cursor chain with NO exit wall across the lane; 60–140 m long; lanes 14–40 m wide)
GRAND HALL (pillar rows, two galleries, a chandelier orb, ramps between levels) · STAIR (a sweeping stair never walked: banister slide, a wall-run wall beside it, pillar kicks, a lip at the top to corner-boost) · SHAFT (a vertical climb or drop: kick walls, lips every ~6 m, an orb, a drop chute) · ROOF (open sky, gaps to bhop, parapet lips, chimneys to kick) · CHUTE (a 20–35° slide 60–100 m with lips and pads, ending in a hop or glass) · COURT (an open yard, exits at different heights, fuel units) · GALLERY (long wall-run walls with kick fins, a catwalk high line) · DROP-IN (a fall onto a slide into a hop; the recovery wall kick for a missed line) · BANK (an S-curve inside the section with banked walls so speed survives the turn) · BRIDGE (a span with a gap; a fast line under or over it).

## Dimensions (from the ladder: jump 9 rises 1.6 m; corner boost to 18 m/s rises ~6.5 m; a bhop at 18 m/s covers ~16 m, a run jump ~7 m)
Ceilings ≥ 8 m in halls, ≥ 12 m where lips stack; lanes ≥ 6 m; gaps 6 m = main line, 9–14 m = fast line; lips 0.9–1.8 m above the approach (reach 0.45 m above the feet after the jump's rise); kick walls 3–6 m tall; wall-run walls ≥ 12 m long; chutes 20–35°; drops ≤ 12 m and always onto a slide or a pad; vertical corners for edge boosts are real 90° outside corners at least 4 m tall.

## The API (`public static partial class Course` in LevelSectionsCourse*.cs — NOT LevelSections, whose old Hall/Chute/Gallery clash; chains still open with LevelSections.Begin(seed, floorY), set zones with LevelSections.Utopia/Under/Office/Works(c), close with LevelSections.Finish; every level builder uses exactly these)
`Cursor Hall(root, config, c, float len, float halfW, float height, int galleries = 2, bool chandelier = true)` · `Cursor Stair(root, config, c, float rise, float len, float halfW)` · `Cursor Shaft(root, config, c, float rise, float halfW)` (rise < 0 = a drop) · `Cursor Roof(root, config, c, float len, float halfW, int gaps)` · `Cursor Chute(root, config, c, float drop, float len, float halfW)` · `Cursor Court(root, config, c, float len, float halfW, int exits)` · `Cursor Gallery(root, config, c, float len, float halfW, float height)` · `Cursor DropIn(root, config, c, float drop, float halfW)` · `Cursor Bank(root, config, c, float len, float halfW, float swing)` · `Cursor Bridge(root, config, c, float len, float halfW, float gap)` · `void Checkpoint(root, c, string name)` · `void Fuel(root, config, c, EnemyKind kind, Vector3 at, string line)` (a unit on a named line). Each space seeds its own fuel and fast lines deterministically (Begin(seed) as before), dresses by zone, and returns Exit() with NO north wall and NO door; the next space lands on the cursor's floor level.

## What the room system's removal changes
RoomLoop (gates, ROOM ACTIVE plates, loops, SetDormancy by room) → CourseFlow (checkpoints, segment names, the respawn-moving rule). Enemy dormancy by DISTANCE (Resting beyond ~120 m or unseen). CombatBaseline per SEGMENT (checkpoint to checkpoint). LevelWeld groups by 40 m cell (no rooms). DebugHUD's room line → the segment's name. MusicDirector's combat mood → units engaged within ~40 m. LevelLint flags any sealed space. Chapter 3's rooftops (L11–13) are already open: bring them to the rules (no gates), do not rebuild.
