
**The style, HARD LINE** (the user asked 2026-09-29 for "a precision overhaul... meaningful detail on every small pixel and block... a distinct art style"):
1. Three tones on every block: the surface; a BLACK BEAD (5 cm, 3.5 cm on small blocks) on every outside edge; ONE LIT LINE (1.6 cm, unlit, low intensity) just inside the top edges of anything you can stand on, in the zone's light — Utopia warm white, Works amber, Underbelly ember, Office cold white, Surface/roofs gold (inset 0.62 m on roofs so it sits inside the kerb). Cyan and red stay reserved (cuts, danger).
2. No surface is flat: the textures carry seams with bevels, bolts on all four sides of a plate, a quarter shadow line on panels, pits in concrete, a bevel into every tile joint, scratches along and across, ridges that catch light, dust on the lower half of every panel, and a fainter metre line inside the 2 m speed grid.
3. Rooms keep their trims (Articulate: skirting, dado, cornice, soffits, inlays; Works rooms: Densify). The bead pass leaves anything named Wall alone so the two never double up.
4. Light comes from fixtures, strips, lamps and lines, never from nowhere.
5. Silhouettes: fantasy and sci-fi at once (see [[city-art-direction]]).

**Where it lives:** `LevelKitPrecision.cs` — `LevelKit.Precision(fallbackZone, LevelSections.Last)` runs in GameBootstrap right after CoplanarFixer (and in Editor/LevelPreviewTool). It walks every solid cube (MeshRenderer with a Cube mesh and a collider, Lit shader, 1.2–400 m), classifies it flat/upright by the local axis nearest world up, beads the top edges (and the vertical edges of uprights), and lines the top edges of flats whose name says walkable (Floor, Roof, Ledge, Landing, Bench, Catwalk, Deck, Lintel, Rim, Cross, Top, Step, Platform, Gallery, Balcony, Tier, Terrace, Stage, Walk, Bridge, Span, Ramp, Slab). Skips: Wall, Pad, Hazard, Glass, Sign, Beacon, Text, Emblem, Portal, Door, Milk, Orb, Water, Lamp, Light, Neon, Strip, Pinch, Kerb, Rail, Cart, FarGround, Ash, Ground. Also: flats that are not a room floor get their underside edges beaded (catwalks, roofs and decks are seen from below), slabs 4 m and over get a 9 cm bolt head on each top corner, and uprights get a dark 14 cm shadow strip round their foot (fake contact shadow on whatever they stand on). Everything it makes is welded into "HardLine" meshes under a HardLine object, decor, no colliders; beads are centred ON the edge (half proud) so nothing is coplanar. The zone per block comes from the section layout's room z-ranges. Texture detail lives in LevelKitTextures.cs (Albedo, PanelHeight, PlateHeight, GrooveHeight, ConcreteHeight) and LevelKit.Grid().

**2026-10-07:** the architecture and palette moved to DARK DECO ([[deco-direction]]); HARD LINE stays as the rendering rule (the black beads are the ink line of the style; the lit lines take the zone's warm colours).
**Why:** after playing many games the user found the levels lacked polish; they want every block to carry detail and the game to look like itself.

**How to apply:** new kit pieces should name walkable slabs with one of the walkable words so they get their line, and name glow/hazard things with a skipped word. Do not add beads by hand where the pass will (it would double). Not play-tested by me. Related: [[visual-overhaul]], [[environment-density]], [[coplanar-faces]], [[ui-house-style-spec]].
