## The idea
A level is a DISTRICT: a grid of city BLOCKS with STREETS and ALLEYS between them, the start at one corner and the finish at the far one, and MANY ways between — along the streets (open ground: traffic, lamp standards, trams, the elevated), down the alleys (fire escapes, dumpsters, washing lines, lips and kick walls), and THROUGH the establishments that fill the blocks, each enterable from two to four sides at different heights (a door, a window, a roof hatch, a cellar hatch, a skylight) so a route can cut through a hotel's lobby and out of its ballroom's balcony, drop through a speakeasy's trapdoor into its cellar and up the coal chute into the alley, cross a theatre's flies, run the department store's escalators, climb a bank's columns to its roof. Several routes should be viable at run speed and the fastest ones need tech. Open areas (a plaza, a market, a rail yard, a park, a hotel's palm court) sit at the junctions so the player sees three ways on.

## Rules
No gate anywhere; every establishment is a pass-through with its own lines; lanes ≥ 6 m on the main streets, alleys may be 3 m (a tech line); checkpoints are 3D VOLUMES (CourseFlow reads boxes, not z lines), one per junction; respawn moving along the route's forward; the finish is reachable by at least three distinct routes; fuel on lines, never waves; the course dimensions hold inside; every walk-into-able thing solid; the city's fabric and traffic around; nothing linear on the HUD (the segment name is the junction's). The Cursor chain may still lay the spine, but the district builder lays blocks on a GRID around it and the spine is only one of the ways.

## The API (LevelSectionsDistrict*.cs; `public static partial class District`)
`DistrictPlan Plan(int seed, int blocksX, int blocksZ, float blockW, float blockD, float streetW)` · `void Build(root, config, plan, Zone zone)` (streets, alleys, junction plazas, the fabric beyond) · `void Place(plan, int bx, int bz, Establishment kind, int rotation)` · each `Establishment` builds inside its block with `Entrances` recorded (position, height, facing) and its interior `Lines` · `Checkpoint(root, name, Bounds volume)` · `Route(plan, from, to)` (the authored main route for the respawn forward) · `Fuel` as the courses'. Builders (Level1..5) compose: the Ward/cloakroom opening, then a plan with named establishments on named blocks, the finish block, checkpoints at junctions.
