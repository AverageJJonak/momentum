## The idea
The House's staff are CLOCKWORK MANNEQUINS: department-store window dummies given clockwork by the company. Smooth enamel shells (cream, charcoal, oxblood livery painted on, brass buttons), visible BRASS BALL JOINTS at the shoulders, elbows, hips and knees (the "integrated joints" read), articulated brass hands with painted white gloves, a WIND-UP KEY turning slowly in the back (life), a GLASS HEAD (a bell jar) with the brass escapement and balance wheel ticking inside and the cyan glow of the mainspring = the weakspot (unchanged hitbox: the head sphere), a brass name plate on the chest, period props by role (tray, luggage trolley hook, torch, whistle, truncheon, lantern, ticket punch, shovel). They move like mannequins: stiff, precise, small steps, the head turning in clicks, a tick-tock in the gait (the procedural gait is tweaked, not replaced). They never bleed: cuts open the shell and spill gears and springs (the ragdoll copier carries the parts; debris = gears).
