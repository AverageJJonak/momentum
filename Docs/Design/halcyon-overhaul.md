## 1. The company
HALCYON CONSOLIDATED runs everything a person of quality could want: the Halcyon House (the tower: its flagship hotel and head office), the theatres, the department stores, the casinos, the racetrack, the ocean lines and the railways, the newspapers, the power, and the Works and collieries that feed it all. It owns the city the way a hotel owns its guests. Its staff are AUTOMATONS in livery; its guests never check out (the pods, the Ward). The voice is courteous, possessive, plural: "the House". `LevelKit.Company` stays "HALCYON"; `CompanyFull` becomes "HALCYON CONSOLIDATED".
Taglines (signs, menus, holograms): EVERYTHING, BEAUTIFULLY · THE HOUSE PROVIDES · A ROOM FOR EVERYONE · SERVICE WITHOUT END · YOUR PLEASURE IS OUR BUSINESS · ALWAYS AT YOUR DISPOSAL.
Corruptions (the glitch flips, red): EVERYTHING IS OURS · THE HOUSE ALWAYS WINS · NO ONE CHECKS OUT · SERVICE IS COMPULSORY · YOUR BUSINESS IS OUR PLEASURE · YOU ARE AT OUR DISPOSAL.
Chapters become ACTS: ACT I · THE HOUSE (the civic levels), ACT II · THE WORKS (the colliery and foundries), ACT III · THE HEIGHTS (the rooftops). Levels become SCENES in the programme (SCENE 2). Level titles may be re-flavoured where the old title is cyberpunk, as long as the new title describes the place (e.g. INTAKE → RECEPTION; UNDERBELLY · THE SUBLEVEL → BELOW STAIRS; ASCENSION · THE SPIRE → THE PENTHOUSE); scene and builder names never change.

## 2. The player's kit (classes keep their enum names; only what they ARE changes)
Run rule unchanged: a run starts bare-handed; the first rack (now THE CLOAKROOM) is where a case is taken or walked past. Labels (`RunState.ClassName`): Fists → KNUCKLES, Swordsman → CANE, Marksman → DECK, WeaponsMaster → THE SET. Everyone carries the WHIP (the grapple).
- **KNUCKLES** (Fists.cs, ParryArmFists.cs): brass knuckles on grey suede gloves. Jab, lunge, TACKLE-THROUGH unchanged. Feel: snappier jab pose, the tackle leans the camera into the shoulder.
- **THE CANE** (ParryArm.cs, BladeDesign.cs, SwordStrike.cs unchanged mechanics): a gentleman's sword cane. Brass derby handle, dark lacquered malacca shaft ringed with brass ferrules; drawn, the ferrules UNLOCK INTO THE CHAIN — the existing segments, tethers and links ARE the ferrules — and the tip is the ferrule spike with the cyan edge (cyan = cut, the law). Parry pose = the cane held across the body; strikes = the chain whip as today. Feel: the draw is a flourish, the cuts crack.
- **THE DECK** (Bow.cs, ParryArmBow.cs; every mechanic kept, class names kept: `Bow`, `Weapon.Kind.Bow`): fifty-two razor-edged steel cards from the House's casino. Hold to FAN (the draw: one card, then three), release to FLICK; a card inherits your velocity, so the faster you run the deeper it cuts (SpeedDamage as today); a weakspot hit is an ACE (execute). The projectile is a flat steel card spinning about its normal with a thin trail; a burning state is a card lit at the corner (amber ember). Stamps keep their meaning.
- **THE BLUNDERBUSS** (NEW: Blunderbuss.cs, ParryArmBlunderbuss.cs; the stub Blunderbuss.cs already holds the HUD contract): a brass-mouthed antique off the Trophy Room wall, THE SET's third weapon, `Weapon.Kind.Blunderbuss`, Switch cycles cane → deck → blunderbuss. Two barrels. Strike fires: a cone (half-angle 22°, reach 7 m; a fan of SphereCastAll like the sword's sweep, walls block) hits every enemy in it for blunderbussDamage 70 × falloff (1.0 inside 3 m → 0.4 at 7 m); a weakspot inside 3 m is an execute (KillMethod.Blast). The BLAST JUMP: the shot kicks you blunderbussKick 9 m/s along −aim (attributed: MoveType.BlastJump, detail "BLAST"; via the motor's Launch/AddImpulse, never a raw velocity write). LADDER LAW: after the kick, horizontal speed is capped at max(current, run) × PerfectTierMultiplier(current) (the fists' lunge cap) so no blast ever beats a perfect bhop chain; the vertical kick from the ground aiming straight down is ~1.3× a jump. Skill gate: a shot within ±50 ms of a jump's apex is a PERFECT BLAST (×1.12 kick, detail "+x m/s  PERFECT"). After two shots the breech opens: 1.1 s reload, the viewmodel breaks the gun, two shells drop. Every number in MovementConfig (blunderbussDamage, Reach, HalfAngle, Kick, Reload, PerfectWindow, PerfectMult). GameFeel: `boom` + `shot` buckets, shake, FOV kick, roll kick; recorded clips only. Style: BlastJump is a freshness move; Blast kills count in variety.
- **THE WHIP** (GrappleAbility.cs visuals only): the grapple dagger and rope become a leather bullwhip: a tapered dark line to a knotted cracker; the yank and swing are untouched; sounds from the `leather`/`belt` buckets. Labels GRAPPLE → WHIP in the HUD and the tutorial.
HUD contract for the new weapon (DebugHUD reads these, nothing else): `Blunderbuss.Instance` with `Held`, `Shells` (0–2), `Reload01` (1 = ready), `Open`, `Readiness`, `LastFiredAt`. The UI shows CANE / DECK / BLUNDERBUSS in the weapon line where it showed SWORD / BOW.

The player fights STAFF, not robots in general. Behaviour, AI, hitboxes, colliders, timings, damage and tells (gold windup, cyan weakspot, red unparryable, amber own light) never change; the ragdoll copier must still find every part. ## 6. Hard rules (unchanged, restated)
Cyan = cut only; gold = parry/reset/flow; red = danger; amber = a unit's own light; no magenta/violet/green/pink/teal. Perfect bhop beats every class trick at every speed; every velocity change attributed. HUD shows state, never instructions. No Random or per-frame allocation in OnGUI; UIScale everywhere; never UIScale.Label inside GUI.BeginGroup. Config never read in Awake/OnEnable of runtime-added components. No synthesized SFX; every new action gets a GameFeel hook from recorded buckets. Colliders never on detail geometry; walk-into-able things solid. The company mark never glitches. Never "Sukuna"/"Fuga". Compile with Tools/compile.ps1 before reporting.

## 2c. RUNS (user 2026-10-12: "lets make the game run style instead of level to level selection, there should be a separate menu part for specific level selection with an option to pick ur weapon at the start, but each run should start at level 1 and save")
A RUN is the game: NEW RUN always starts at Level 1 bare-handed (the Ward, the Cloakroom); the class is chosen at the first cloakroom and locked; finishing a level advances the run (RunState.Level = the next scene index, TotalTime, Score, per-level results) and SAVES to PlayerPrefs; CONTINUE RUN (the first menu entry when a run exists: "ACT I · SCENE 3 · CANE · 12:40") loads the run's next level with its class; dying/quitting mid-level keeps the run at that level (checkpoints are not saved: a continue restarts the level); the finish of the last level ends the run with a RUN COMPLETE card (total time, score, class) and records the best run; abandoning (NEW RUN over an existing one) asks once. FREE PLAY = THE PROGRAMME page: any level reached by a run (the existing unlock rule) can be launched directly with a CLASS PICKER first (KNUCKLES / CANE / DECK / THE SET): the class is Forced (never saved), the cloakroom in that level builds sealed ("ISSUED"), and free-play results never touch the run (level bests still count). Menu: CONTINUE RUN · NEW RUN · PROGRAMME · PRACTICE RANGE · ORIENTATION · SETTINGS · QUIT; the register slip shows the run. Pause → THE DIRECTORY keeps the run saved. Tutorial/range force THE SET as today.
